Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a private, single-user desktop tool that uploads and schedules the Shorts of one YouTube channel, Ninguém Te Contou, operated by the tool's owner. It is not offered or distributed to anyone else.

How it works

  1. The owner produces each Short and reviews it on a local review page.
  2. Only after manual approval, the tool uploads the video to the owner's channel through the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), as private with a scheduled publish time.
  3. It reads the authorized channel's basic info (channels.list) to confirm the right channel is connected.

By using this tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
