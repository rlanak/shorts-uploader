Novela Uploader

Novela Uploader is a private, single-user desktop tool that posts the episodes of an animated short series, Bambu de Ouro, to one TikTok account (@gessiane.frota) operated by the tool's owner. It is not offered or distributed to anyone else.

How it works

  1. The owner produces each episode and reviews it on a local review page.
  2. Only after manual approval, the tool sends the video to the owner's TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, either as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a direct post, labeled as AI-generated content.
  3. It reads the authorized account's basic info (display name and posting options) to confirm the right account is connected.
