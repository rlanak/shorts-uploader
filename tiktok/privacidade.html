Privacy Policy — Novela Uploader

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

What the tool does

Novela Uploader is a private desktop tool that runs on its owner's computer and posts videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It has a single user: the owner.

Data accessed, used and stored

  • user.info.basic: read the authorized account's display name and avatar to confirm the right account is connected.
  • video.upload and video.publish: send videos (with captions written by the owner) to the authorized account, as drafts or direct posts, and read their processing status.
  • The access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's computer (a local file). The publish ID and status of each post are kept in a local log.

Sharing

No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties. The tool does not collect data about any other person. No cookies or tracking are used, and there is no advertising. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions.
  • Deleting the local token file removes all stored credentials immediately. Locally stored data (publish IDs and status) is deleted on request or when the tool is uninstalled, and is never kept longer than 30 days after access is revoked.
